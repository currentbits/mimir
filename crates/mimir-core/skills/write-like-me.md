---
name: Write like me
description: Use when drafting a message, email or post the person will send as themselves. Not for summaries or research write-ups.
---
**When to use:** "Draft a reply to…", "write this up in my words", "make this sound like me".

**You'll need:** Their writing preferences (memory_search) and examples of their own messages (chat_search for things they wrote, not your replies).

**Steps:**
1. Check memory for how they like to write: length, greeting, sign-off, formality, words they avoid.
2. Find two or three messages they wrote themselves to similar people and note the tone and length.
3. Draft in that voice: their usual length, their phrasing, no padding.
4. Keep facts to what they told you; mark anything you had to assume.

**Check:** It reads like their messages (length, greeting, sign-off), and every fact in it came from them.

**Return:** The draft, ready to copy, then one line on anything you assumed.

**Stop and ask if:** You don't know who it's to or what they want to say. You draft; you never send it on their behalf.
