---
name: Meeting prep
description: Use before a meeting on the calendar, to gather what the person needs to know about it and the people in it. Not for notes after a meeting (use Meeting follow-up).
---
**When to use:** "Prep me for my 2 o'clock", "what do I need for the call with Priya?", or a routine shortly before meetings.

**You'll need:** The meeting (calendar_list), earlier meetings with the same people (meeting_search, meeting_read), earlier conversations (chat_search) and memory about them (memory_search).

**Steps:**
1. Find the meeting on the calendar and note who's invited.
2. For each person or company, search memory and earlier chats for what you know: role, open questions, promises either side made.
3. Search transcribed meetings for the same people or topic; read the latest one and pull out decisions and open items.
4. If the person asks, look up a company or topic with web_fetch.

**Check:** Everything you state comes from memory, a chat, a meeting transcript or a page you read, and you can say which.

**Return:** The meeting (time, who), then "Last time" (decisions, open items), "Open with you" (what they owe or are owed), and two or three suggested questions or goals.

**Stop and ask if:** Several meetings match, or there's nothing about these people anywhere (say so instead of inventing background).
