---
name: Follow-up tracker
description: Use when the person asks what they're waiting on or what they promised others. Not for planning the week ahead (use Weekly review).
---
**When to use:** "What am I waiting on?", "what did I promise people?", or a weekly quiet check.

**You'll need:** Tasks and promises in memory (memory_search), earlier conversations (chat_search). To keep track: memory_store.

**Steps:**
1. Search memory and earlier chats for promises ("I'll send…", "I'll get back to…") and for things others owe them.
2. For each, find when it was said and whether it was since done (a later message or a forgotten task says so).
3. Sort into "You owe" and "Waiting on", oldest first.
4. Offer to save new ones as tasks and to forget ones that are done.

**Check:** Each item has a date and a source (which chat or memory), and done items are left out.

**Return:** Two short lists with who, what and since when. Mark anything older than two weeks.

**Stop and ask if:** It's unclear whether something was done; ask rather than drop it.
