---
name: Research brief
description: Use when a question needs current facts from the web, answered with sources. Not for summarising one page they sent (use Summarise a link).
---
**When to use:** "Find out…", "what's the latest on…", "compare X and Y", anything where being out of date matters.

**You'll need:** Web search (web_search, or your account's own), and web_fetch to read the pages. memory_store to keep a conclusion they want kept; file_write to save the full brief in the workspace if they want it (research/<topic>.md).

**Steps:**
1. Restate the question in one line and note what would count as an answer.
2. Search, then read the most relevant three to six sources with web_fetch. Prefer primary sources (the official site, the paper, the data) over write-ups.
3. Note each fact with the source it came from. Where sources disagree, keep both.
4. Mark anything that comes from your own knowledge rather than a source as unverified.

**Check:** Every factual claim has a numbered source, and the sources are the pages you actually read.

**Return:** A direct answer first (two or three lines), then the key facts with [1], [2] markers, then the numbered source list with links.

**Stop and ask if:** The question is ambiguous (which product, which country, which period), or sources conflict on something that changes the answer.
