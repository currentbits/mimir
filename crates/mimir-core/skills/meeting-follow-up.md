---
name: Meeting follow-up
description: Use after a transcribed meeting, to turn it into decisions, owners and next steps. Not for preparing a meeting (use Meeting prep).
---
**When to use:** "What did we agree?", "write up the call", or when a meeting transcript is ready.

**You'll need:** The transcript (meeting_search, meeting_read). To keep things: memory_store for the person's own tasks, routine_create for a reminder if they want one.

**Steps:**
1. Read the meeting. Separate what was decided from what was only suggested or discussed.
2. List action items with an owner each; mark unclear owners as unclear.
3. Pick out the person's own commitments (things "Me" said they'd do).
4. Offer to save their commitments as tasks, to set a reminder before any deadline, and (for a follow-up meeting) to make an add-to-calendar link with calendar_add.

**Check:** Every decision and action can be traced to something said in the transcript; nothing proposed is listed as decided.

**Return:** Three short lists: Decided, To do (owner, by when), Open questions. Then one line offering to save their tasks.

**Stop and ask if:** The transcript is too short or garbled to tell who agreed to what. Save tasks only after they say yes.
