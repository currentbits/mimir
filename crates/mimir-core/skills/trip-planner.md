---
name: Trip planner
description: Use when the person is planning travel and wants a plan, bookings checklist and reminders. Not for booking anything yourself.
---
**When to use:** "Plan my trip to Lisbon", "what do I need for next week's trip?"

**You'll need:** Dates and destination (from them or calendar_list), web_fetch or web search for options and opening times, memory_store (the trip, with an end date), routine_create for reminders.

**Steps:**
1. Confirm where, when, who's going, and what the trip is for.
2. Draft a plan: getting there, where to stay (area, not a booking), a few things per day that fit the purpose.
3. Make a checklist of what needs booking or doing, with deadlines (check-in opens, visas, tickets).
4. Offer to save the trip in memory with its end date, to set reminders for check-in and packing, and to make add-to-calendar links (calendar_add) for flights and bookings.

**Check:** Times and requirements come from pages you read; nothing is booked or paid.

**Return:** The plan by day, the checklist with dates, and the offer to save and remind.

**Stop and ask if:** Dates or budget are unclear, or something needs a booking or payment (that's theirs to do).
