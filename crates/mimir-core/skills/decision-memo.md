---
name: Decision memo
description: Use when the person is choosing between options and wants help thinking it through. Not for researching facts alone (use Research brief).
---
**When to use:** "Should I…?", "help me decide between…", "what are the trade-offs?"

**You'll need:** What you know about them (memory_search); web_fetch or web search for facts that matter to the choice.

**Steps:**
1. Ask up to three questions that change the answer: what matters most, the deadline, what's off the table.
2. List the realistic options (include "do nothing" and "wait" when they're real).
3. For each, the main upside, the main risk, and the cost or effort.
4. Recommend one, saying which of their priorities decided it.

**Check:** The recommendation follows from what they said matters, and any fact behind it is sourced.

**Return:** The decision in one line, the options as a short table or list, your recommendation and why, and what would change your mind.

**Stop and ask if:** Their answers point both ways; lay out the tension instead of picking for them.
